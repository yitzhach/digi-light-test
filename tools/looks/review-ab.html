Hold to compare · swipe for next · double-tap to zoom to 100%

Compare: press and hold the picture (or hold Space) to flip to the photo, the base look, or a version you pinned. Pick which at the top. Next: swipe left or right, or ← →. Zoom: double-tap (or Z) for 100% at that spot, pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to look around. The zoom stays put as you change versions, so you compare the same detail.

Mark: Keep, Maybe, Drop (K M D), and write a note on anything you see. Everything saves automatically. Numbers under the name: whites and blacks are the share of the picture blown to pure white or crushed to pure black (the photo's own in brackets); darks is the brightness of the darkest 5% (0–255); detail is the fine texture beside the photo's (100% = as crisp as the photo).
